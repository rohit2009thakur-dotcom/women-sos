Empowerment & Defense Knowledge

Rights guaranteed by the Constitution and practical self-defense techniques.

📜

Right to "Zero FIR"

You can lodge an FIR at ANY police station in India, regardless of where the incident occurred. Police cannot refuse on jurisdictional grounds.

🌙

No Arrest After Sunset

Under Section 46(4) of CrPC, women cannot be arrested after sunset and before sunrise, except under exceptional judicial magistrate orders with a female officer present.

🔒

Right to Complete Privacy

Victim statements can be recorded privately before a female police officer or magistrate. Identity disclosure is strictly prohibited under IPC Section 228A.

💼

PoSH Act (Workplace Protection)

Every establishment with 10+ employees must maintain an Internal Complaints Committee (ICC) to investigate harassment complaints within 90 days.

📧

Virtual / Email Complaints

If unable to visit in person, women have the right to file written police complaints via registered email or speed post to the Commissioner or SP.

🩺

Free Medical Aid

Under Section 357C of CrPC, all hospitals (government or private) are legally mandated to provide immediate free first aid and medical treatment.

✋

1. Palm-Heel Strike (Not a Fist)

Never punch with a closed fist (can break fingers). Drive the base of your open palm upward directly into the assailant's nose bridge or chin.

🦵

2. Groin Knee Drive

Grab attacker's shoulders or clothes with both hands to stabilize yourself, and drive your knee with full hip power upward into their groin. Run immediately.

🔄

3. Wrist Grab Escape (The Lever)

Don't pull back directly. Rotate your wrist toward their thumb (the weakest point of their grip) and jerk your forearm sharply upward toward your opposite shoulder.

👀

4. Eye Gouge / Throat Notch

If pinned against a wall or ground, thrust stiff fingers into eyes or the soft hollow notch at the base of the throat. This triggers an involuntary reflex release.

🔑

5. Improvised Everyday Shields

Keys protruding through knuckles, perfume/deodorant spray into eyes, heavy bag swing, or steel water bottle jab make lethal deterrents.

🗣️

6. The "Fire!" Shouting Rule

Psychology shows shouting "Help" often causes bystanders to hesitate. Yelling "FIRE!" or "POLICE!" instantly compels people to turn around and investigate.
