01. Problem Statement

Why Conventional Safety Apps Fail in Real Emergencies

⏱️

Network & Auth Latency

Commercial apps require OTP logins, password sync, and cloud servers. If the user is in a low-reception alley or zero-data zone, the app stalls.

👀

Aggressor Retaliation

If a suspicious driver or stalker sees a victim opening a brightly branded "Police SOS" app, panic escalates into immediate physical aggression.

📢

Absence of Audible Deterrents

Silent SMS alerts don't scare off an attacker in a deserted street. An instant, ear-piercing crowd alarm is vital to disorient assailants.

Key Takeaway: An emergency tool must be instantaneous, zero-login, and psychologically smart. Response Time Target: < 1 Second
02. Design Philosophy

The Visual Metaphor: "Zero-Gravity of Fear"

Defying Gravity, Defying Fear, Living in Complete Calm.

Inspired by Studio Ghibli's serene world-building, our platform is anchored by the imagery of a 75-year-old grandmother floating weightlessly in her thatched village hut, surrounded by burning diyas and utensils.

Ancestral Protection: Grandmothers in Indian culture symbolize unshakeable courage, resilience, and rooted community wisdom.
The Zero-Gravity Symbol: Just as gravity weighs down physical matter, fear weighs down women's dreams and mobility. When safety is guaranteed, fear has zero weight.
अभय (Abhaya): The Floating Sanctuary • Studio Ghibli Style
Artistic integration bridges high technology with emotional reassurance and human dignity.
03. System Architecture

Client-Side Zero-Latency Technical Stack

⚡

Pure Client-Side

Zero backend server round-trips. Executes entirely in browser memory. Instant response even on throttled 2G networks.

🔊

Web Audio API

Synthesizes sound directly via browser `AudioContext` and `OscillatorNode`. No external audio files to download.

📍

Geolocation API

Direct browser GPS queries with `enableHighAccuracy: true`. Formats real-time Google Maps pinpoint coordinates.

🔒

Zero-Tracking Storage

Emergency contacts stored strictly on the local device via HTML5 `localStorage`. Full DPDP & GDPR privacy compliance.

Protocol Handlers: Deep links to OS-native dialers (`tel:112`), SMS clients (`sms:?body=`), and WhatsApp dispatch (`wa.me/?text=`).
04. Core Feature #1

Rapid SOS Dispatch with 5-Second Safeguard

Engineering the Perfect Emergency Trigger

  • ✓ 5-Second Cancellation Window: Prevents false alarms when the phone is in a handbag or pocket.

  • ✓ Dynamic Google Maps Pin: Automatically packages live latitude/longitude into a universal link.
  • ✓ Multi-Channel Broadcast: Pre-populates emergency message drafts to both WhatsApp and SMS.

05. Core Feature #2

Web Audio Deterrent Suite (Siren & Whistle)

📢

Synthesized Police Siren

Uses a sawtooth waveform oscillator sweeping continuously between 700 Hz and 1300 Hz with an exponential ramp every 400 milliseconds. Emulates authentic high-decibel emergency response vehicles.

OscillatorType: 'sawtooth' | Freq: 700Hz - 1300Hz
🔊

Staccato Whistle Burst

Generates a high-pitch sine waveform ramping from 2600 Hz to 3200 Hz in rapid 3-stage bursts. Pierces ambient background traffic noise to alert nearby citizens.

OscillatorType: 'sine' | Freq: 2600Hz - 3200Hz
Acoustic Advantage: Because audio is generated programmatically, it requires 0 kB of asset streaming and plays instantly upon button click.

07. Core Feature #4

Journey Guardian & Safety Circle

⏱️

Journey Guardian (Transit Check-In)

Designed for students commuting between the campus, library, coaching institutes, or hostels late at night.

1. User sets expected transit duration (e.g. 10 or 30 minutes).

2. Active timer runs in the background with continuous display.

3. If the user fails to tap "I'm Safe" before expiry, an audible alert chimes and triggers automatic emergency SOS protocol.

🤝

Private Safety Circle (Contact Manager)

Allows users to configure up to 5 primary trusted contacts (Parents, Siblings, Friends, Roommates).

• Stored 100% locally in device memory using HTML5 `localStorage`.

• Direct speed-dial and individual WhatsApp SOS options.

• Absolute privacy: No contact data leaves the browser.

Empowers women with proactive transit monitoring rather than only reactive post-incident measures.
08. Legal Empowerment

Constitutional Rights Vault & Emergency Helplines

Statutory Rights (India)

📜 Right to Zero FIR: Lodged at ANY police station regardless of jurisdiction.
🌙 Section 46(4) CrPC: Women cannot be arrested after sunset and before sunrise.
💼 PoSH Act 2013: Mandatory Internal Complaints Committee (ICC) in every college & office.
🔒 Section 228A IPC: Complete prohibition of identity disclosure for victims.
